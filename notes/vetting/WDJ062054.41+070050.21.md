# WDJ062054.41+070050.21 (TIC 167394689) — unconfirmed

Verdict: **unconfirmed** (2026-11-12, backlog 28). DA (SIMBAD/MWDD; Gaia XP DA P = 0.89), TeffH 10,347 K, G = 16.27,
d = 88.9 pc. Sector 87 (20 s): 878.4 s, 15.1 ppt, 1.34x the 1,000-shuffle 0.1 % FAP amplitude, both halves; Sector 33:
nothing above threshold (strongest 865.6 s, 8.7 ppt = 0.82x). CROWDSAP 0.075–0.078: the WD is ~8 % of the aperture flux,
so the corrected amplitude is a ~1 ppt raw signal that any of the four neighbours within 41" (G 13.0–16.2) could carry.
A cool ZZ Ceti with variable modes is plausible but not shown.

## 2026-11-13 — ZTF targeted test and literature (verificador)

Verdict: **known** (published), not new. Huang et al. 2026 (ApJS 284, 72; arXiv:2605.03323, Appendix A table "White
dwarf oscillators in this work", arXiv HTML) list TIC 167394689, Sector 87, 98.358 c/d (= 1,138.4 µHz, 878.4 s),
15.635 ppt, among their 24 new pulsating white dwarfs. Our 2026-11-12 check missed it (abstract only).

- ZTF (`data/labs/ztf_target/WDJ062054.41+070050.21/result.json`, figure [ztf_target_WDJ062054.41+070050.21.png](../../figures/ztf_target_WDJ062054.41+070050.21.png)):
  407 g + 605 r epochs. The 7.1" neighbour (G 16.2, BP−RP 1.05) is resolved as a separate source.
- Coherent 878.4-s mode: **not detected** on the white dwarf (p = 0.21, 0.59–0.62× threshold) nor on the neighbour
  (p = 0.16). Injections: A50 = 10.1 ppt, A90 = 12.4 ppt, so a stable 15-ppt mode would have been seen. The
  validation set shows why this is weak evidence: long-period (≳ 550 s) modes of known pulsators are rarely
  recovered, presumably because they are not coherent over the 7-yr ZTF baseline.
- **Excess scatter on the white dwarf only**: std of the ZTF flux 35.6 ppt (g) and 27.6 ppt (r) against median
  errors of 13.0 and 14.8 ppt (ratio 2.7 and 1.9); the neighbour, 0.5 mag fainter in g, is at its error level (16.3/14.9
  and 13.4/13.8 ppt). Season-averaged spectra of the white dwarf are flat (10.4–10.9 ppt rms amplitude in g across
  300–8,000 µHz), as expected when sparse sampling folds fast incoherent variability into white noise. Excess
  ≈ 33 ppt rms in g and 23 ppt in r (larger in g, as for pulsations). This supports the TESS attribution to the
  white dwarf (which the CROWDSAP of 0.08 had left open), but it is not a period.
- Gaia DR3: no variability information (`phot_variable_flag` NOT_AVAILABLE). Jestin et al. 2026 table:
  Variable = False, Periodic = False.
