# WDJ194549.13-153135.63 (TIC 423077063) — unconfirmed hot-DAV signal

Verdict: **unconfirmed** (2026-11-12, backlog 28). DA (O'Brien et al. 2023, VizieR J/MNRAS/518/3055: Teff 12,590 ± 40 K,
log g 8.422), d = 30.9 pc, G = 14.67, near the hot edge of the strip. Only Sector 92 (20 s), CROWDSAP 0.41: peak at
195.74 s, 2.39 ppt = 5.2 <A>, 1.08x the 1,000-shuffle 0.1 % FAP amplitude (2.21 ppt); halves 2.85 ± 0.54 and
2.01 ± 0.50 ppt; a second peak at 194.11 s (2.0 ppt, below threshold). Below the 1.1x that Romero et al. 2024 ask for a
single period, so not claimed. Worth a second look if TESS re-observes it. SIMBAD: WD*, references are catalogues
and O'Brien et al. 2023.

## 2026-11-13 — ZTF targeted test (verificador)

Verdict unchanged: **unconfirmed**. ZTF neither confirms nor rules out the 195.74-s signal.

- ZTF (`data/labs/ztf_target/WDJ194549.13-153135.63/result.json`, figure `dashboard/img/ztf_target_WDJ194549.13-153135.63.png`):
  372 g + 600 r epochs (30-s exposures), 8 seasons per band. Three sources within 10": the white dwarf and two faint
  red neighbours (G 18.1 at 7.5", G 19.7 at 8.4"), all resolved.
- 195.74 s (5,108.715 µHz): window maximum 0.76–0.81× the 0.1 % false-alarm threshold; p_freq = 0.007–0.020
  (it depends on which 300 control frequencies are drawn: 0–6 of 300 controls exceed it). Not a detection. The fitted
  amplitude is 0.4 ppt in g and 3.0 ppt in r; a ZZ Ceti mode is larger in g than in r, so this is not what a real mode
  would look like. 194.11 s (second TESS peak, 2.0 ppt): p = 0.047, not detected. Neighbours: p = 0.11–1.
- Sensitivity (injections on the white dwarf's own ZTF data): 50 % recovery at 2.7–3.0 ppt, 90 % at 4.2 ppt. The
  TESS amplitude (2.39 ppt) sits just below A50, so ZTF could only have seen it if the mode were stable and slightly
  larger in g. Upper limit: a coherent 195.74-s mode stronger than ~4 ppt over 2018–2025 is excluded.
- ZTF scatter equals the photometric errors (std/err = 1.0 in g, 1.1 in r): no excess variability.
- Gaia DR3: `phot_variable_flag` = VARIABLE; G scatter 15.1 mmag over 20 transits (5.2× expected), classifier best
  class WD (score 0.67). A 2.4-ppt mode cannot produce 15 mmag of scatter, and ZTF shows none, so the Gaia flag is
  not explained by this signal; not used as evidence.
- Jestin et al. 2026 (VizieR J/A+A/712/A243/tablea1): listed, Variable = False, Periodic = False. Not in Huang et
  al. 2026 (which covers Sector 92).
