# WDJ142213.99-332351.75 (GALEX J142213.9-332352, TIC 270350156) — candidate new ZZ Ceti

Found 2026-11-12 by `wdplanets/labs/zzceti.py` (backlog 28). Verdict: **candidate** (single sector, period ambiguous).

- G = 16.15, d = 75.2 pc; Gaia XP class **DA** (P = 0.94, Jiménez-Esteban et al. 2023), Teff 11,324 ± 152 K,
  log g 7.95; our TeffH 11,567 K, log g 7.99: inside the strip.
- TESS: only Sector 102 at **120 s** (Nyquist 4,167 µHz), CROWDSAP 0.31. Peak at 3,896.3 µHz = **256.65 s**, 7.9 ppt,
  1.36x the 1,000-shuffle 0.1 % FAP amplitude (5.8 ppt); both halves 7.0 ± 1.4 and 8.8 ± 1.5 ppt. Because the peak is
  close to the Nyquist frequency, the true frequency may be the reflection at 2 × 4,166.7 − 3,896.3 = 4,437 µHz (225 s).
- Neighbours within 21": G 18.5 at 6.8", G 16.8 at 12.1" (no BP−RP), G 16.2 at 12.7" (BP−RP 0.92). The 12" stars are
  as bright as the WD and inside the same TESS pixel, so attribution is weaker than for J2102−1234; the check step
  flagged it "contaminated" because one neighbour has no Gaia colour.
- Literature: SIMBAD (Harvard mirror, 20" region with all object types): WD* only, no Pu*/ZZ*/V*; bibcodes listed on its page: 2018MNRAS.480.4505J, 2018yCat.1345....0G, 2020yCat.1350....0G, 2023MNRAS.518.5106J. Not in the TIC tables of Romero et al. 2022 (S1-39) or 2024 (S40-69) - the star had no 120/20-s data before Sector 102, after their searches. Web search found no variability report.
- To confirm: 20-s data in a future sector (resolves the Nyquist ambiguity) or ground-based photometry.
